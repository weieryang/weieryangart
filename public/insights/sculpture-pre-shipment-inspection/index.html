How to Inspect a Custom Sculpture Before Shipping

Published November 24, 2026By WEIERYANG
Before the crate is closed

Inspect a custom sculpture while the workshop can still show its surfaces and assemble its parts. Start with the drawing revision and finish sample you approved. Review the overall form, ask for close-ups of the areas that will be hidden and match removable parts to the crate list. Give the workshop a written decision for each open item before you release it for packing.

What can you see in the workshop photos?

Look at the wing tips in the first photograph. The three-quarter view shows the space between them and the position of the raised forelegs. In the side view, one wing covers much of the other. That angle shows where the short feathers meet the long flight feathers. For a sculpture facing a hotel entrance, mark the approach direction on your site plan and ask the workshop to repeat the photograph from that angle.

If a wing on your own design travels separately, give the joint a number on the drawing. Ask for one photograph of the joint assembled and another showing its matching marks before wrapping. This gives the installer a way to identify the pair when the crates arrive.

How do you release a piece for packing?

Name the person who can release the work. Have them record a decision against each item. If a view is missing, identify the exact view to repeat, its owner and its due date. Where your specification calls for a material certificate or an engineer’s inspection, record that document’s reference alongside the photographs.

Example entry. The packing list puts wing joint J-02 in crate B. The trial-fit photo shows its front face, but the match mark is on the reverse. Mark J-02 “Hold”, ask the workshop for a reverse view with both labels visible, then enter the new photo ID and reviewer’s decision in the same row. What should go into your first inquiry?

Send a sketch or reference image, the approximate dimensions, the intended site and any sections you expect to ship separately. Mark one feature you want to see before packing. If the installer already has a drawing or a crate-label convention, include its revision or a sample label so the workshop can scope the records you need.

Frequently asked questions

Can a buyer inspect a custom sculpture remotely?

Agree on the drawings, sample and photo angles before the review. Ask the workshop to show any missing views on a live call and save a labeled still. If the contract requires a physical inspection, arrange that visit before the piece is wrapped.

What if the finish looks different on screen?

Ask for a new photograph with the approved sample and sculpture together in the same light. If the difference is still material to the decision, keep the item open and arrange a physical comparison with the designated reviewer.

Who should receive the pre-shipment records?

Give the fabrication team and the authorized buyer the signed decision sheet. Give the receiving and installation team the matching joint map, labeled photographs and packing list so they can identify the parts after delivery.
