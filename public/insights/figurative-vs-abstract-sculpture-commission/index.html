Figurative vs Abstract Sculpture for a Custom Commission

Published November 18, 2026By WEIERYANGEnglish procurement guide
Direct answer

A figurative sculpture gives the viewer a subject to recognize. An abstract sculpture draws attention to shape, space or movement. A single work can do both. To brief an OEM sculpture maker, show where people will stand and mark the feature that must read from that spot. The two workshop photographs below show how differently that instruction can look.

What should visitors see from the approach?

Start with a photograph taken from the doorway, path or turn where the artwork first appears. Mark a second spot where people can stand close to it. For a hotel entrance with two approaches, mark both on a plan. A front view that shows a face clearly may show only a narrow profile from reception. An opening in a loop may disappear against a column behind it.

Write a viewing instruction the fabricator can check. For a portrait, that might be “the lifted hand and face must read together from reception.” For an abstract form, try “the opening between the curves should show the wall behind it from the entrance.” These are example instructions for a new brief.

How do you brief a sculpture that reads both ways?

Look at the leaf-face workshop study. The outline reads as a face, while the leaf-like pieces create a second pattern. A buyer commissioning an original piece with a similar dual reading could mark the facial outline on the distant view and the gaps between elements on a close-up. The same two marked views give the designer and the buyer a shared basis for the first model review.

Send a brief that a supplier can price

Copy these prompts into the project description and attach the sketch or artwork you are authorized to use. An early concept is enough to ask what design development would cost.

  • From the first viewpoint, visitors should see … Attach a marked site photograph with an arrow showing the direction of travel.
  • Up close, these details matter … Label them on a sketch. State which details the supplier may develop and which have already been approved.
  • The space available is … Give height, width and depth with units, the base location, and any known access restrictions.
  • Please price these items separately … List concept development, model or sample, fabrication, packing, freight and site work as needed. Give the destination and identify who will review the next drawing.

Once you have chosen a design, use the OEM drawing and sample approval checklist to record the version, the reviewer and the decision. For a hotel or resort brief, include the arrival plan from the hospitality sculpture guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is figurative sculpture in art?

A figurative sculpture depicts a recognizable subject, such as a person or animal. A maker can simplify the face or exaggerate a gesture and still keep the subject legible. For a commission, mark the features the viewer needs to recognize.

Can one sculpture be both figurative and abstract?

Yes. A face framed by leaf-like shapes can read as a portrait across a room and as a pattern up close. Specify both viewing positions and show which reading matters at each one.

Which type is easier to quote from a photograph?

A photograph can start either discussion. Ask the supplier to identify what the image leaves open. For a portrait that might be the back of the head. For an abstract loop it might be the depth of an opening seen from the side. Provide a second view or ask for the missing view to be developed as a separate scope item.

Does a simple abstract shape usually cost less to make?

A smooth loop can require tight control of its profile, seams and reflections. A portrait can concentrate work in a few highly recognizable details. Give both options the same size, finish reference, delivery location and approval scope, then compare what each quotation includes.
