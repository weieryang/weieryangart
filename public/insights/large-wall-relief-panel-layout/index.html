How Do You Divide a Large Wall Relief Into Panels?

Published November 25, 2026By WEIERYANG
Start with the whole wall

Draw the full relief on the measured wall first. Mark the features that must remain whole, the parts that can be divided and the largest section the building can receive. Give every proposed panel a front-view label. Two quotations can then be compared against the same pattern and joint positions.

Which lines should a joint avoid?

Look at the raised face in the gold-coloured relief. A vertical line down its centre would draw attention to the split. On the blue circular work, the curved surface lines lead towards the central opening. A change in their direction across a joint could be more noticeable than the gap itself. Use the two works to mark where a division would interrupt the design. Use the drawings from your own supplier to establish how the finished panels join.

On the full elevation, trace a proposed joint and mark the distance from a fixed end of the wall. Show the adjacent texture or motif on both sides. Ask for a drawing of the entire composition with the panel outlines over it so the designer can move a joint before pricing is fixed.

Choose what a guest should see at the join

For a continuous pattern, review two neighbouring edges together under the planned light. Ask for the finish direction to be marked on the back of each sample. If the joint is part of the design, show its intended width and colour on the drawing and ask the workshop what it can maintain over the actual panels. The finish sample and the joint sample answer different questions.

One public example of a composition built from repeated parts is Gordon Huether’s Super Duper. The Sunnyvale collection describes how folded elements make up the wall artwork. The decision for your site is whether the viewer should read the parts individually or see one continuous image. View the Sunnyvale public art record.

Get a quote that includes the work on site

Send the wall width and height, a photograph taken square to the wall, the delivery opening and an annotated elevation. Ask each bidder to itemise the relief panels, layout template, panel identification and any finish work after assembly. If the wall includes a cabinet or a service hatch, name the person responsible for keeping it accessible.

Frequently asked questions

Does a large relief need a centre seam?

The seam plan follows the composition, transport limits and site access. Ask for two layouts if a centre seam would cross a face, lettering or another focal feature.

How do we avoid installing mirrored or reversed panels?

Number panels on an elevation viewed from the room. Add an up arrow and a matching label to each panel and crate. State explicitly when a drawing shows the back.

What should two suppliers quote on the same basis?

Give both the same wall elevation, finish reference, proposed seam treatment, site access dimensions and division of installation work. Ask each to flag alternatives separately.
