How to Approve a Bronze Patina for a Hotel Lobby Sculpture

Published November 28, 2026By WEIERYANG
How does a hotel team approve the finish?

Ask the fabricator for a labelled patina sample showing a raised edge and a recess. Compare it beside the proposed lobby finishes in daylight and under the evening lighting scene. Record the accepted colour range, sheen, protective coating and reviewer before the workshop finishes the sculpture.

View the patina beside the actual lobby materials

Put the sample beside the stone, timber or metal closest to the proposed work. A bronze edge can look bright against a dark wall and quiet beside polished metal. Record the viewpoint from reception and another from a guest seat. Ask whoever specifies the lighting to call up the evening scene with its final settings.

A rendering can communicate the intended mood. A physical sample lets the designer tilt the same surface through the light and examine where the highlight breaks. The two photographs above show the lighting question in real interiors. The supplier’s material declaration identifies the metal proposed for your commission.

Use one sample to record four separate decisions

Name the sample so the hotel, fabricator and purchasing agent can all refer to the same piece. For a surface with deliberate variation, mark a light and a dark area the designer is willing to accept.

Agree who keeps the sample through handover

On a hotel project, the art consultant may approve the colour while the operator chooses how the surface will be cleaned. Give each reviewer the same sample ID. Ask the fabricator for care instructions specific to the proposed metal, patina and coating, especially where guests or cleaning carts can touch it.

If the designer requests a brighter edge after approval, photograph the change next to the control sample and obtain a new approval for that area. Keep a labelled reference with the operator for later repairs. Getty’s technical guide describes the layers that can make up a bronze surface. This is a reason to record the finish and coating together.

What to send for a hotel bronze sculpture quote

Provide the lobby elevation, installation city, approximate artwork dimensions, expected viewing distances and the interior finish schedule. Identify a person who can review physical samples. If part of the work will be outside or near a pool, mark the exposure separately so the studio can propose an appropriate construction, finish and care plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is a flat bronze colour chip enough?

It can set a colour direction. When the design has curves, folds or relief, ask for a shaped sample so the team can review changes in reflected light.

Should we match the existing lobby door handles?

If a match matters, identify one actual handle or finish sample and view it beside the proposed patina. Record how close the match needs to be under the relevant lighting.

What changes for a resort courtyard?

State whether the sculpture is sheltered, exposed to rain, near salt or within splash range. Request a written material and care proposal for those conditions.
