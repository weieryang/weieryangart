Discuss my sculpture project Prepare for a first meeting

What should I bring to a first sculpture project meeting?

A marked-up site photo, an approximate height with units and the delivery city are enough to start. Mark the main viewing position. If the piece must pass through a doorway or gate, include its clear width. A sketch is welcome while detailed drawings are still in progress.

What to ask the fabricator

  • Shape. Ask to see the silhouette from the marked approach. An opening visible from above can disappear at eye level.
  • Finish. For polished metal, ask for a finish sample and a close view of a joint beside the surrounding surface.
  • Delivery. If the sculpture travels in sections, ask where they meet, what the joint will look like and who will assemble it on site.

Hotel sculpture procurement

Translate the design idea into a package the hotel team can approve.

A custom hotel sculpture supplier should make the interfaces visible before production: who approves the form and finish, who engineers the local support, how the object enters the building, what is trial-assembled, how the surface is protected and where supplier responsibility ends.

  • Design package: site response, scale, approved viewpoints, material route, finish sample and coordinated drawings.
  • Engineering package: internal structure assumptions, segmentation, weight, base reactions, lifting information and local consultant review.
  • Quality package: sample sign-off, workshop hold points, finish photographs, trial assembly and packing inspection.
  • Delivery package: export packing, shipping marks, route survey inputs, installation method and handover boundaries.

What to judge before choosing a supplier

A reliable supplier makes the hidden delivery route visible.

For large outdoor sculpture, the risk is rarely only the shape. The quotation becomes more reliable when the supplier can discuss site scale, water or coastal exposure, material grade, internal structure, surface finish, segmentation, packing volume, lifting access and who will coordinate local installation.

01 Site review

Drawings before style

Good proposals ask for site photos, plans, approach sequence, viewing distance, climate, water, lighting and maintenance facts before confirming the form.

02 Material proof

Samples before quotation

316L stainless steel, bronze, stone, corten and hybrid routes should be selected for exposure, touch, reflection, patina and cleaning access.

03 Fabrication proof

Structure before finish

Large sculpture needs visible evidence of segmentation, frame logic, weld control, repeated member alignment and workshop quality control.

04 Delivery path

Packing before shipping

Export packing, lifting points, base connection, installation documents and local-team coordination should be planned before production is locked.

Image-led project evidence

What do the construction photos show?

Follow the flying-bird sculpture from its exposed frame to the alignment of the wing sections. Before requesting a similar scale, ask which joints will be made in the workshop and which will be completed at the site.

Best-fit projects

Match the supplier's scope to the setting.

A lobby sculpture, resort entrance and outdoor water feature need different design inputs and delivery evidence. Choose the relevant setting, then review the fabrication partner against the site conditions and approved artwork brief.

  • Hotel arrival sculpture: approach sequence, porte-cochere scale, night lighting, guest photography, base and installation window.
  • Resort and water feature sculpture: reflection, splash radius, drainage, stainless grade, pool access and maintenance clearance.
  • Public art sculpture: structure, touch, wind, durable finish, foundation assumptions, public safety and installation coordination.
  • Garden and estate sculpture: planting height, seasonal light, stone or bronze warmth, privacy, path views and long-term weathering.
Material and review documents

The fastest first reply comes from useful project facts.

Before requesting a price, prepare the facts that change the sculpture: site drawings, site photos, target scale, material direction, destination country, schedule, installation access and whether local contractors will handle foundation and lifting.

Which outdoor sculpture materials are usually reviewed first?

Common review routes include stainless steel, bronze, stone, corten steel and hybrid material systems. Confirm the grade, finish and sample according to exposure, touch, reflection, maintenance and installation access.

Does the quotation include shipping and local installation?

These items must be defined in the project quotation. Confirm export packing, freight, insurance, import arrangements, unloading, lifting, foundations, local engineering review and installation responsibilities; overseas installation guidance does not by itself include a local installation crew.
