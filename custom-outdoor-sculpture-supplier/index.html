What to judge before choosing a supplier

A reliable supplier makes the hidden delivery route visible.

For large outdoor sculpture, the risk is rarely only the shape. The quotation becomes more reliable when the supplier can discuss site scale, water or coastal exposure, material grade, internal structure, surface finish, segmentation, packing volume, lifting access and who will coordinate local installation.

01 Site review

Drawings before style

Good proposals ask for site photos, plans, approach sequence, viewing distance, climate, water, lighting and maintenance facts before confirming the form.

02 Material proof

Samples before quotation

316L stainless steel, bronze, stone, corten and hybrid routes should be selected for exposure, touch, reflection, patina and cleaning access.

03 Fabrication proof

Structure before finish

Large sculpture needs visible evidence of segmentation, frame logic, weld control, repeated member alignment and workshop quality control.

04 Delivery path

Packing before shipping

Export packing, lifting points, base connection, installation documents and local-team coordination should be planned before production is locked.

Best-fit projects

Commercial search traffic should land on a clear project route.

This page is built for buyers comparing suppliers for custom outdoor sculpture, hotel arrival sculpture, resort landscape sculpture, water feature sculpture and public art fabrication. Each route needs different proof, but the review path is consistent.

  • Hotel arrival sculpture: approach sequence, porte-cochere scale, night lighting, guest photography, base and installation window.
  • Resort and water feature sculpture: reflection, splash radius, drainage, stainless grade, pool access and maintenance clearance.
  • Public art sculpture: structure, touch, wind, durable finish, foundation assumptions, public safety and installation coordination.
  • Garden and estate sculpture: planting height, seasonal light, stone or bronze warmth, privacy, path views and long-term weathering.
Material and review documents

The fastest first reply comes from useful project facts.

Before requesting a price, prepare the facts that change the sculpture: site drawings, site photos, target scale, material direction, destination country, schedule, installation access and whether local contractors will handle foundation and lifting.

Supplier FAQ

Questions buyers ask before commissioning custom sculpture.

What should I send before asking for a custom sculpture quotation?

Send site photos, plan drawings, target size, preferred material direction, destination country, schedule, water or lighting conditions, and any installation constraints.

Does WEIERYANG only design sculpture, or can it support fabrication and delivery?

WEIERYANG can support design development, structural coordination, sculpture fabrication, export packing and overseas installation guidance for suitable projects.

Which materials should be reviewed first?

Common review routes include 316L stainless steel, bronze, stone, corten steel and hybrid material systems, selected according to exposure, touch, reflection, maintenance and installation access.

Are the project images completed-project photographs?

Construction photographs are labelled as verified construction-phase or work-in-progress records. The plaza image is labelled as a concept-stage study and is not presented as a completed-project photograph.

Have a real site or tender package?

Send drawings, site photos, scale, material direction, country and timeline through the private commission brief. For related decisions, read the overseas installation checklist and the water feature material checklist.
