Choosing a Hotel Lobby Sculpture That Works from Three Views

November 18, 2026By WEIERYANG
Before you request a quote

Trace the walk from the entrance to reception, then look back from a lounge seat. Mark what guests should see from each position. An open centre that frames the reception desk on arrival can disappear when seen side-on. Ask the fabricator for those three views before approving the form and its orientation.

Three ways to read a freestanding form

From the WEIERYANG project archive. Select a photograph for the full view.

Mark the three views on one floor plan

For the entrance view, include the desk sign, the ceiling lights and the floor line in the same photograph. For the desk view, stand where a guest waits, then mark anything the object would hide. For the lounge view, take the picture from a seat. A base that looks quiet at standing height can dominate the view from a low chair.

Choose which part of the form is allowed to disappear

For the looped piece, a front elevation gives the impression that both openings stay visible. A quarter turn changes how the openings overlap. In a supplier review, mark one as essential and the other as optional. For a solid stack, make a different decision: mark the outside edge that should remain readable against the background. Use the same background photographs when comparing both proposals.

Make the signed view reproducible on site

Give the approved drawing a front arrow and label its direction with a fixed feature, such as the centre of reception. Dimension the artwork and the plinth separately. Ask who supplies the plinth, where the finish joint falls and who signs off the building connection. If the interior layout changes, the marked reference point gives both teams a reason to revisit the orientation.

Send the three-view lobby brief

Buyer questions

Which view should lead if reception and the entrance disagree?

Name the primary arrival view in the brief. Ask for one alternate orientation that protects the important feature in the secondary view, then have the hotel team choose between the two marked drawings.

What should the OEM quotation include before the plinth is built?

Request the approved front direction, the artwork footprint, the plinth footprint, the visible joint and the party responsible for the fixing interface. Keep finish approval and building review as separate sign-offs.
