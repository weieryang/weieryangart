Cast Bronze vs. Bronze Resin Sculpture for Indoor and Outdoor Projects

Published November 28, 2026By WEIERYANG
What is the difference between bronze and bronze resin?

In cast bronze sculpture, the sculptural metal is a bronze alloy cast from molten metal. A bronze resin or cold-cast bronze sculpture uses bronze powder with a resin binder, often in a surface layer over a backing. Ask the supplier to identify the material in the body and the surface construction in writing.

Read the material line before comparing the price

A quotation that says only “bronze finish” tells you how the supplier expects the work to look. It leaves the construction open. Ask what material forms the sculptural body, whether it is hollow, what backs the surface and what coating is applied. A metal casting can be hollow. A resin casting can contain real bronze powder at the surface.

One commercial bronze powder data sheet from AeroMarine describes use with resin for cold-cast effects. A supplier offering that type of finish should be able to name the resin system proposed for your project. Ask for a labelled sample or an agreed section photograph if two finished samples look similar.

Compare both options on the same sculpture

Give both bidders the same size, pose, base, destination and access information. Ask each to complete the blank columns in the downloadable comparison sheet.

Choose a construction for the intended site

An indoor object on a display plinth and a piece within reach of visitors in an exposed courtyard have different requirements. List sunlight, water exposure, cleaning method and the likelihood of handling. Ask what happens if the surface is scratched and whether a repair will expose a different material below.

For an outdoor public-art brief, request a written statement about the proposed system, support interface and care for that location. Have the site team review the installation information for the selected option. Colour, a photograph or the apparent weight of a sample alone cannot resolve those choices.

Ask for a comparable written offer

“Please quote both constructions for the attached model. State the metal alloy or the resin and powder system, describe the backing, and quote the same base, finish sample, packing and delivery location. List the expected packed weight and the repair method for each option.” This gives purchasing a basis for discussing a price difference with the designer.

Frequently asked questions

Does cold-cast bronze contain real metal?

It can contain real bronze powder mixed with resin. Ask where the powder is present and which material forms the body of the proposed piece.

Does a hollow sculpture mean it is bronze resin?

No. A metal bronze casting can also be hollow. Ask for the material description, a construction view and the supplier’s statement of how the shell was made.

Can bronze resin be specified for outdoors?

That depends on the particular resin system, finish, exposure and support. Request the supplier’s written suitability and care information for the actual site before specifying it.
