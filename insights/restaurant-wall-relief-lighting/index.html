Choosing a Wall Relief for a Restaurant Booth or Host Stand

November 18, 2026By WEIERYANG
Before you request a quote

Review a restaurant wall relief twice: first from a seated guest’s eye level, then from the host’s standing position. Use the intended light direction in both views. A raised edge can make a pattern readable from across the room while sending a bright reflection toward the nearest table.

Photograph the wall from the booth

Put the seat, tabletop and any pendant between the camera and the wall in the first photo. On the elevation, mark the back of the banquette and the outer edge of the artwork. Ask for the relief’s deepest point at the nearest occupied seat. That gives the interior team something to check against the way guests and staff use that corner.

Test a piece of the relief under the evening light

A flat colour chip helps select a colour. A sample containing a ridge, a recess and a finished edge shows how that colour looks across the actual profile. Rotate the sample under the proposed light and compare the view from the host stand with the view from the seat below. Keep a photograph of the selected orientation with the approved sample.

Resolve the panel breaks before the wall finish is ordered

Mark the wall finish joints, fittings and access panels on one elevation. Decide whether a joint should align with a raised line or fall in a quieter area. Ask how a panel could be reached for cleaning or replacement after the booth goes in. If staff must reach across a fixed table, test that reach with a cardboard outline during the fit-out review.

Buyer questions

Which sample is most useful for a shiny or textured relief?

Ask for a representative section containing a high point, a recess and the proposed finish. Review it under the planned light from both the closest seat and the host position.

Can we get an initial quote before the lighting plan is final?

Share the available wall envelope, relief depth, intended viewing positions and a provisional light location. Ask the supplier to list lighting-dependent approval work separately so it can be revised when the fixture is selected.
