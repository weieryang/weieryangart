How High Should a Hotel Lobby Sculpture Plinth Be?

Published November 25, 2026By WEIERYANG
Measure from the finished floor

Choose the elevation of the feature guests should see, then subtract its height above the bottom of the artwork. The difference gives a starting plinth height. Mark the plinth footprint and the widest part of the artwork on the lobby plan, and test the result from a lounge chair and the entrance.

Choose a point on the artwork

In the photograph of the intersecting loops, the centre of the opening frames the room beyond. The blue circular sculpture has its own stem and base, which already lift the opening before the artwork reaches a plinth. Lifting either work by the same amount would have a different effect. Circle the feature you want a seated guest to see before specifying the pedestal.

For example, suppose the centre of an opening sits 750 mm above the bottom of a sculpture. The designer wants it 1,200 mm above the finished lobby floor. A 450 mm rise would place it there. If the artwork sits on a separate 50 mm mounting block, the visible plinth contributes 400 mm. These figures illustrate the arithmetic. Try the proposed level in the actual room before ordering.

Try the height in the furnished lobby

Tape the footprint on the floor and use a light cardboard outline to mark the opening at the proposed height. Take one photograph from the lounge chair and a second from the arrival route. Include the reception sign or doorway in each frame so the reviewer can tell what the artwork reveals or obscures. Shape Arts also recommends considering seated and standing viewing positions when planning art displays. Read its exhibition access guidance.

Who supplies the plinth?

Ask for separate scope lines for the artwork, visible plinth and connection between them. If a local millworker supplies the plinth, request a drawing of the mating surface from the sculpture supplier. Give the named site reviewer the proposed weight, projection and floor conditions, and agree who checks stability and installation before either part is ordered.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a standard pedestal height for a hotel lobby?

The useful height depends on the artwork, the feature being framed and the room. Use the intended display elevation and a mock-up to choose a project-specific height.

What happens if the artwork already has a base?

Measure from the bottom of the supplied base to the feature you want to frame. Put its footprint and fixing interface on the drawing so the plinth supplier allows for it once.

What should I send to discuss a plinth?

Send a side view with one known dimension, the intended display height, a lobby plan or photograph, and the delivery city. Indicate whether the hotel or the sculpture supplier will provide the plinth.
