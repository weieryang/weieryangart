Water Feature Sculpture OEM Quote Checklist

Published November 9, 2026By WEIERYANGEnglish procurement guide
Direct answer

Request a water feature sculpture OEM quote against one marked-up section drawing. Identify where the sculpture ends and the basin, waterproofing, piping, electrical supply and controls begin. Assign each connection to the parties designing, supplying, installing and checking it. Use the same drawing revision and scope rows for every bidder.

Mark how the artwork meets the water

Start with the water path and the sculpture footprint. A reflecting pool sculpture might stand on a dry plinth beside the basin, sit on a support in a wetted zone or carry water through an outlet in the artwork. Mark the normal waterline, splash area, mounting surface and any pipe or cable crossings on the same section. Ask the fountain designer to confirm the operating conditions that the sculpture drawing should use.

For a custom fountain sculpture with an integrated outlet, show the point where the fountain contractor’s pipe meets the fabricated assembly. Identify any removable panels needed to reach that connection after installation. For a dry plinth beside a separate pool, show the pool edge and the art fixing detail so the two packages can be priced separately.

Assign six interfaces before comparing quotes

Use the table as a scope schedule. On each row, enter the drawing number and revision, the named parties responsible for design, supply, installation and verification, and the item each quote includes or excludes. Record unresolved decisions with a named owner and a due date.

Compare two quotes on the same scope

Consider an illustrative fountain sculpture with a removable nozzle. Quote A includes the artwork and terminates at a connection on the sculpture. Quote B includes the artwork, the connection and the nozzle. Put the nozzle, matching pipework, access panel and wet test on separate rows. Ask each bidder to mark each row as included, separately priced, supplied by the site team or awaiting a decision. If the nozzle is selected later, record who will approve its envelope before the artwork drawing is released.

Send changes in writing with a drawing revision. A moved outlet or revised basin level can affect more than one scope line. Review the affected lines with both packages before requesting revised pricing.

Close the drawing and handover gaps

Before the fabrication release, have the project team confirm the latest section, agreed connection points and the responsibilities for any site-supplied components. For the installation release, confirm access and lifting with the site team. Assign someone to record the agreed water-effect and lighting checks at handover, then pass the final interface drawings and relevant equipment instructions to the operator.

The workshop photograph below shows curved metal components during production. For your own sculpture, ask the fabricator to mark which components are accessible for inspection and which connections the site team will complete.

Copy these fields into your request for quotation

  • Site and application, such as a resort reflecting pool or public fountain court.
  • Drawing numbers and revisions for the artwork, basin and relevant services.
  • Water relationship and marked connection points for the sculpture.
  • Design, supply, installation and verification parties for each interface.
  • Inclusions, separately priced options and items supplied by others.
  • Open decisions, approval dates and the required inspection or handover records.

Send your marked-up section and scope schedule through the private sculpture project brief. Use the water feature material checklist to prepare the separate finish and exposure discussion.

Frequently asked questions

Does a fountain sculpture OEM quote include the pump and controls?

Check the equipment list and scope schedule. Confirm the pump, circulation equipment, controls and connections as separate lines, with a named supplier and a named party responsible for the site checks.

Can a concept rendering be used for initial pricing?

A concept can support an indicative discussion. Give the bidders the same assumed sculpture envelope and water relationship, then request a revised quote when the basin section, connection points and exclusions are confirmed.

Who is responsible for sealing around the sculpture base?

Assign the sealing detail and verification to named parties in the project scope. The sculpture fabricator and basin team should coordinate the base, sleeves and other penetrations against the same section drawing.

Which documents should reach the fountain operator at handover?

Agree the record list in the quote. It can include final interface drawings, equipment instructions, inspection and wet-test records, and access notes for the serviceable parts included in the project.
