Which Drawings Do You Need to Fabricate an Outdoor Sculpture?

Published November 25, 2026By WEIERYANG
What to send first

Start with the artist’s sketch, one dimension with units and a site photo marked with the main approach. For an outdoor sculpture with openings or folds, add a second view. Those documents can support a preliminary quote while the artist and fabricator identify which details still need decisions.

What can the fabricator read from the sketch?

On the plaza sculpture, one gold-coloured ring passes in front of another. A front elevation shows their outline and leaves their front-to-back spacing open. In the entrance photograph, the opening frames a building, so the camera position also matters. If the turn matters to the artist, mark it on a side view or on two photographs taken from marked positions.

Give the sketch a revision name and keep it with the site photo. A line on a drawing can mean an opening, a change of finish, a joint or simply a shaded edge. Mark which meaning the artist intends. A workshop can then explain what each interpretation would change in its price.

Separate the estimate from permission to fabricate

Ask the workshop to put its assumptions beside the estimate. If depth is still open, request a price based on a stated depth and ask what would trigger a revision. Reserve a separate approval for the fabrication drawing that fixes the geometry. Record who may approve the appearance and who will review the base and site interface.

For one public commissioning example, New York City’s Public Design Commission lists scale and orientation among its drawing requirements. The project owner can identify the applicable review process at the actual site. See the commission’s artwork checklist (PDF).

Example wording for the first inquiry

“Please price the loop shown in sketch B, measured from the top of the plinth. Preserve the opening from the arrival arrow in the site plan. The depth is open. Please show your assumed depth, identify the surfaces included in the finish sample and list the drawings you would return for approval.” Add the delivery city and name the person reviewing the artwork.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request an estimate with a hand sketch?

Yes. Add one known dimension, the units, the project location and a site photo. Ask the fabricator to list assumptions and the decisions needed for a firm price.

Which file controls if the sketch and 3D model disagree?

Name the governing revision in the brief. Mark the discrepancy and ask the artist or designated approver to resolve it before releasing the affected part for fabrication.

Who provides the foundation drawing?

Ask the buyer’s site team and the fabricator to identify who supplies the interface information and who completes the site design. Record both responsibilities in the quote.
