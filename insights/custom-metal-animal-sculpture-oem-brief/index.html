Custom Metal Animal Sculpture OEM Quote Checklist

Published November 14, 2026By WEIERYANGEnglish procurement guide
Direct answer

A quote for a custom metal animal sculpture should identify the animal and approved pose, the size and finish, the support points, and who supplies the base, packing and installation. For an outdoor hotel entrance or public plaza, send the supplier your preferred views and a marked-up site photo so they can price the form and its interface with the site together.

Specify the silhouette from the visitor’s side

Show the animal from the angles visitors will actually see. For a horse viewed from a drop-off lane, mark which side should read clearly as vehicles approach. For a flying bird in a plaza, show the wing spread from the main pedestrian approach and from below. If only one view has been drawn, tell the supplier where the other views are open to development.

The layered horse fabrication record shows how the outer edges and gaps of horizontal profiles define the body. If you are commissioning a similar open form, identify the views where the chest, legs and tail need to read clearly. Ask for those views in the approval model. For a solid curved form, agree how the profile and reflections will be reviewed under the intended lighting.

Identify which details need a finish sample

A polished finish swatch shows sheen, but a small flat piece will leave questions about a curved flank or a seam near the animal’s face. Point to those locations in the drawing and agree what should be represented by a formed sample or a selected first-piece area. For a layered animal, ask where the visible edges will be checked and how finish will be reviewed across adjacent layers. The guide to models, finish samples and first pieces explains what each approval covers.

Compare two quotes for the same pose

Imagine a horse at a hotel entrance where the buyer wants a single, uninterrupted view of the neck and head from the porte-cochère. One supplier prices the body as a single shipping piece. Another proposes a removable neck for access. Ask both to price to the same approved silhouette, show where any joint will be visible, and list the largest packed dimension against the route. Require both quotes to identify who reassembles the piece, who checks the finished joint and whether that work is included.

Use the same comparison for a broad bird or projecting tail. Request a drawing that gives the location of the split, a reference for alignment at assembly, and a list of sculpture-side components and local work. The Middle East bird landmark construction record shows how real site access and the assembled silhouette are part of the same review.

Send a short brief with your first inquiry

Copy these fields into your project description and attach your current drawing or reference image. Approximate values are useful when clearly marked as preliminary.

  • Subject and pose — Animal, stance or gesture, priority views and artwork reference.
  • Size and location — Target overall height and span, country and city, photos and any drawing of the receiving surface.
  • Appearance — Desired metal or finish, nearby lighting and outdoor exposure, where relevant.
  • Quote scope — Fabrication, base interface, packing, delivery and installation responsibilities to be priced or listed separately.
  • Dates and approvals — Requested arrival date, decision maker and which details still need approval.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a custom metal animal sculpture quote with a sketch?

Yes. Send the sketch, the intended viewing angles, an approximate size, and site photos. Flag unconfirmed details as pending and ask the supplier to separate preliminary scope from items that depend on the approved model or site review.

How do I specify a reflective finish for a stainless steel animal sculpture?

Name the surface you want to review, such as the face, flank or wing, and show where it will be seen. Ask the supplier to record the proposed alloy and finish, provide a representative finish sample and agree how visible seams and reflection will be checked.

Does the sculpture quotation include the base or foundation?

These are separate responsibilities to confirm in writing. Ask who supplies the sculpture-side plates, who designs the receiving structure, who installs the fixings and what reference drawing each party will use.

When should I ask for a split shipping option?

Ask early when the finished pose is wider than the delivery or access route can accommodate, or when projecting details need special handling. Compare segment sizes, seam appearance, packing and on-site assembly before choosing an option.
