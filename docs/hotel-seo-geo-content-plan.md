# Hotel SEO/GEO content plan

Updated: 2026-09-24

## Positioning boundary

WEIERYANG should target hotel renovation and hospitality design through the part of the project it can credibly deliver: custom sculpture, dimensional art, material development, structural coordination, fabrication, export packing and installation guidance.

Do not reposition the studio as a general hotel renovation contractor, interior-design practice or art consultant unless those services are formally added and evidenced.

Until provenance is documented, these files are reference-only and must not be copied into production pages. To approve an image for the site, record:

1. Photographer or copyright owner.
2. Permission to publish commercially.
3. Relationship to WEIERYANG: designed, fabricated, installed, supplied, observed or reference only.
4. Project type and location that may be disclosed.
5. Construction, completion or concept status.
6. Any client, architect or designer attribution required.
7. A factual caption and alt text that do not imply unsupported authorship.

## Content rules for SEO and generative search

- Lead with a direct answer, then explain the decisions and evidence behind it.
- Publish original decision frameworks, checklists, boundaries and process knowledge rather than generic trend summaries.
- Keep visible headings, copy, captions and structured data consistent.
- Use one canonical page per intent; do not create pages for trivial keyword variants.
- Keep useful visible FAQs, but do not treat FAQ structured data as a ranking or rich-result shortcut.
- Use descriptive internal links that explain what the destination page answers.
- Label every image as verified construction, workshop evidence, material study, concept study or completed project.
- Do not invent price ranges, lead times, dimensions, certifications, clients or completion claims.
- Keep the page useful without `llms.txt`; discovery still depends on crawlable, indexed pages and strong content.

## Measurement plan

After publication, compare the hotel cluster in Search Console by query, country and landing page. Review:

- impressions and clicks for hotel lobby sculpture, hotel sculpture, hospitality art installation and related long-tail queries;
- pages ranking in positions 8–20 that may benefit from a clearer title, direct answer or internal link;
- image-search impressions for material and construction evidence;
- `commission_view`, `commission_form_start` and successful `generate_lead` events from each article;
- assisted inquiries where the landing page differs from the final commission page.

Do not create additional hotel articles until the first cluster has index and query data or new first-party evidence.
