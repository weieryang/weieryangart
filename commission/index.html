Private sculpture commission brief

WEIERYANG is a sculpture studio and manufacturer based in China. We make custom sculptures from client drawings or develop original designs with designers, contractors, suppliers, developer procurement teams and private owners.

For hotel owners, designers, art consultants and project buyers in the United States and worldwide. Send site drawings, dimensions in feet/inches or metric, destination city and state, finish direction and opening or renovation schedule. Submitted details and files are emailed to the studio for review.
