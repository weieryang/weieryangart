WEIERYANG is a sculpture studio and manufacturer based in China. We make custom sculptures from client drawings or develop original designs with designers, contractors, suppliers, developer procurement teams and private owners. Four starting points for a custom sculpture commission. Choose a spatial direction, review the evidence and tell us about your site. Design, dimensions, finish and delivery scope are developed for each project.
