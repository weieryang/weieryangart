Tree and canopy sculpture for an arrival landscape

WEIERYANG is a sculpture studio and manufacturer based in China. We make custom sculptures from client drawings or develop original designs with designers, contractors, suppliers, developer procurement teams and private owners.

Let the arrival sequence shape the sculpture. Tree and canopy references offer different ways to define a focal point, frame a pedestrian space and connect a piece to the surrounding landscape.

Questions before commissioning

Can the colour and silhouette be developed for our landscape?

Yes. An original design and finish direction can be developed around the architecture and planting. Reference images guide a discussion; they are not a promise to duplicate an existing work.

Can a canopy sculpture provide shelter?

Shelter or usable shade is a separate design requirement. It must be explicitly assessed with loads, drainage, safety and local approvals; decorative references do not establish those functions.
